# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe. We welcome contributions regardless of experience, background, identity, or preferred way of working.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact honestly.
- Critique ideas and work, not people.
- Accept reasonable feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy, consent, intellectual property, and the project's security boundaries.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, unwanted disclosure of private information, or publishing private conversations.
- Deliberate disruption, sustained bad-faith behavior, or targeted abuse.
- Sharing malicious code, credentials, or confidential material.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainer using a private contact method available through GitHub. If no private contact route is available, use GitHub's platform reporting tools rather than posting sensitive details publicly. Do not include credentials, exploit payloads, or sensitive personal data in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports as promptly as reasonably possible, protect the confidentiality of the people involved where practical, and decide on a proportionate response. Responses may include a warning, removal of harmful content, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. The maintainer may decline to act where a report is unsupported or outside the project's scope.

This project is maintained with limited capacity. Do not assume a 24/7 response or a formal appeal process. If the maintainer is directly involved in a report, use an appropriate GitHub platform reporting channel instead.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications. It also applies when someone is representing the project in another space.

## Attribution

This policy is an original, project-specific statement informed by common open-source community practices. It is not a promise of formal moderation capacity or legal adjudication.
