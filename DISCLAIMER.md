# Important Notice and Disclaimer

This template is a practical starting point for working on projects with AI assistance. It is provided as general guidance and software documentation, not as a guarantee that a project will be safe, secure, private, lawful, or suitable for a particular purpose.

## Your project and your choices

You remain in control of your project and are responsible for decisions about what you create, upload, publish, share, and allow others to access. In particular:

- Create your own project repository as **Private** by default when it contains private work or project information.
- Check who can access your GitHub repository, including collaborators and connected applications.
- Check your ChatGPT Project's sharing settings separately. **Only those invited** is the safer default. People who join a shared Project can see its chats, files, and instructions.
- Install the project-specific instructions in the correct ChatGPT Project and check that they were saved. Repository files do not install them for you.
- Do not put passwords, access keys, recovery codes, private keys, or other login secrets in project files, instructions, or chats.
- Before making anything public, review the files, discussions, images, commit details, and other material for private or third-party information and confirm that you have the rights and permission to publish it.

A private GitHub repository does not make a ChatGPT Project private. ChatGPT sharing, account data controls, GitHub access, and connected applications are separate. A private setting reduces exposure but cannot guarantee confidentiality: authorized people or applications may copy, download, screenshot, or otherwise disclose information.

## AI assistance has limits

AI-generated guidance can be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable for your circumstances. You should check important facts, test changes, review suggested code and commands, and seek qualified professional advice where the consequences or legal requirements call for it. Do not rely on this template as legal, financial, medical, cybersecurity, or other regulated professional advice.

The template's checks and instructions reduce avoidable risks; they cannot remove every risk or guarantee that a project meets every law, licence, security standard, or platform requirement. Platform features, settings, limits, and terms can change.
